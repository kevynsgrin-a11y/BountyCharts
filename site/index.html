What will this deck cost you next week?

Every trading card game has a dozen tools that tell you which deck is best. None of them tell you what it will cost to build by the time you finish reading. BountyCharts sits on that intersection — price movement against metagame shift.

Why Riftbound first

Riot's card game launched into the West in October 2025 with an active secondary market, a fast-moving metagame, and a permissive fan-content policy. It is young enough that the price history is thin and the information layer is still forming — which is exactly where this kind of tool is worth building. Other games follow.

What this is not

No buy signals

BountyCharts does not publish buyout alerts, price predictions, or investment advice, and never will. Broadcasting a buy signal to subscribers is the thing that creates the spike — which makes later readers into exit liquidity for earlier ones. This is decision support: what the data says, and where it came from. What you do with it is yours.

Status

Pre-launch. The market research behind this is finished and public — a primary-source audit of the 2024–2026 TCG market, including a unit-economics model and a fact-check of the industry figures most commonly cited. The product is in build.
