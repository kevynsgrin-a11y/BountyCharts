# Fact-Check Ledger

Claim-by-claim audit of *Executive Intelligence Report: Trading Card Game Industry Trajectory, Growth Vectors, and Monetization Strategy (2024–2026)*.

Verified July 2026. Status key: **✅ Confirmed** · **⚠️ Materially wrong** · **🟡 Partly true / overstated** · **❓ Unsubstantiated**

---

## 1. Market sizing

| # | Claim | Status | Finding |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1.1 | Global TCG market "$13.01B in 2024, projected $14.12B in 2025" | ⚠️ | The years are shifted by one. Custom Market Insights — the source the report cites — puts **$13.01B in 2025** and **$14.12B in 2026**. The report ages the entire market forecast forward by twelve months, which makes every derived growth inference optimistic. |
| 1.2 | Market is in a "ruthless consolidation phase" | 🟡 | Directionally defensible but unsupported by the cited data. Analyst 2034–2035 forecasts span **$15.8B to $24.4B** — a 54% spread between firms. That dispersion signals low measurement confidence, not consolidation. Any number in this category should be treated as a marketing artifact, not an input to planning. |

## 2. Pokémon TCG Pocket

| # | Claim | Status | Finding |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2.1 | Launched globally 30 Oct 2024, by Creatures Inc. + DeNA | ✅ | Correct. |
| 2.2 | 60M downloads in first six weeks | ✅ | Confirmed; ~$180M gross over the same six weeks. |
| 2.3 | 100M downloads by late Feb 2025 | ✅ | Confirmed (Sensor Tower). |

| 2.5 | Launch month (Nov 2024) spending $235.3M | ✅ | Confirmed as gross consumer spend. Worth noting the net figure: AppMagic, deducting store commission and tax, reports **$141.4M** for the same month. Both numbers are "true"; they are not interchangeable. |
| 2.6 | >$1.6B revenue in first 18 months | ✅ | Confirmed. Year-one alone was ~$1.3B — ahead of Pokémon GO's year one by ~$245M. |
| 2.7 | Paldean Wonders launch on Pokémon Day (27 Feb 2026) drove $4.7M in 24h | ✅ | Confirmed — a six-month revenue high. The "+486% vs. previous week" multiplier is not independently attested but is consistent with the reported peak. |
| 2.8 | Accounted for ~37% of all Pokémon IP in-app purchase revenue in 2026 | ❓ | No supporting source located. Precise to two significant figures with no attribution — treat as unverified. |
| 2.9 | Trading integration in early 2025 "accelerated search volume" | 🟡 | Trading did launch in early 2025 and did drive search. The report omits that it launched to **significant player backlash** — trade tokens, hourglasses, rarity-matched trades only, high-rarity and promo cards excluded — prompting a public developer response and a planned overhaul by autumn 2025. The search volume the report treats as pure demand signal is substantially *complaint and workaround* traffic. |

## 3. One Piece Card Game

| # | Claim | Status | Finding |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3.1 | "$768 million USD in FY2024" for the One Piece Card Game, +30% YoY | ⚠️ | **Matches no reported figure.** The One Piece Card Game specifically was ~¥26.5B (**~$170M**) in Japan for FY2023–24. Bandai's *entire card-game segment* — all IPs — was ~**$1.99B** in FY2024, +18.1%. The *whole One Piece IP* within Toys & Hobby Japan was ¥94.2B (**~$620M**) in FY2025.3, +56.2%. The report's figure appears to blend IP-wide merchandise revenue with card-game revenue, and its growth rate matches neither series. |
| 3.2 | "Officially outsold Yu-Gi-Oh! by late 2025" | 🟡 | True in a much narrower sense than stated. One Piece outsold Yu-Gi-Oh! **on TCGplayer GMV, in October and November 2025** — a US marketplace measure. On global publisher revenue Yu-Gi-Oh! remained materially larger (¥47.1B vs ¥26.5B). Stated as an unqualified worldwide fact, the claim is wrong. |
| 3.3 | Q1 2026 #3 by GMV on TCGplayer, behind MTG and Pokémon | ✅ | Consistent with TCGplayer's published quarterly rankings (Q4 2025: MTG, Pokémon, One Piece, Yu-Gi-Oh!). |
| 3.4 | Dallas One Piece event, March 2025, 3,000+ participants | ❓ | The first overseas One Piece Day in Dallas is real; the participant count is unattested. |
| 3.5 | Subreddit "over 190,000 weekly visitors" | ❓ | Unverified; sourced to a Reddit thread. |
| 3.6 | "High-Gloss UV coating seals the ink, preventing oxidation" | ❓ | This is vendor marketing copy from a single trade blog, not a manufacturing spec. Not a credible differentiator to build a thesis on. |

## 5. Star Wars: Unlimited / Asmodee

| # | Claim | Status | Finding |
|---|---|---|---|
| 5.1 | Asmodee revenue €1.68B, +23% YoY; TCGs ~60% of net sales | ✅ | Confirmed. TCG share rose from just over 50% the prior year. |
| 5.2 | SWU "explicitly cited as a primary growth driver" of 25.9% organic growth | ⚠️ | Inverts the actual finding. Asmodee's growth is overwhelmingly **distribution** of *other publishers'* games — **72%+ of revenue**, up from 63% — carried by Magic, Pokémon, Lorcana and One Piece. Asmodee's own-IP board game sales **fell**, and SWU's performance was explicitly described as **"normalising"** against its 2024 launch year. SWU is a beneficiary of the TCG wave, not the engine of Asmodee's growth. |

## 7. Monetization mechanics

| # | Claim | Status | Finding |
|---|---|---|---|
| 7.1 | TCGplayer affiliate: 3.5%, 48-hour window, **first-click** attribution | ✅ | **Confirmed against TCGplayer's own documentation.** Worth flagging because most third-party affiliate aggregators incorrectly list this program as last-click. The report is right and the aggregators are wrong. |
| 7.2 | Amazon Associates: 24h, last-click, 1–4% | ✅ | Correct. |
| 7.3 | Deploy "1-Click Buyout API integrations" loading a 60-card deck into a TCGplayer cart | ⚠️ | **Not buildable as described.** See [strategic analysis §2](./tcg-deep-dive-2026.md#21-the-api-hole). TCGplayer stopped accepting new public API applications around late 2024 following the eBay acquisition; as of mid-2026 the developer application path is effectively closed and a Partner API deprecation is documented. There is a supported no-API workaround, but it is not what the report describes. |
| 7.4 | Use Raptive / Mediavine / NitroPay | 🟡 | Correct networks, but the report omits that they are **gated**: Raptive ~25K pageviews/mo, Mediavine "Official" ~25K pageviews (50K sessions), with Mediavine Journey at 1K sessions. A zero-traffic property cannot start here. |
| 7.5 | Gaming vertical supports premium programmatic RPMs | ⚠️ | Gaming is one of the **weakest** display verticals: **$2–6 session RPM**, against $15–40 for personal finance. The $15–50 RPMs quoted for Mediavine/Raptive are blended across all verticals and are not what a TCG site earns. |
| 7.6 | Subscription tiers at $5 / $15 / $49.99 / $149.99 | ⚠️ | Priced **2.5–5× above the category's established leaders**. EDHREC — the dominant MTG Commander data site — starts at **$2/mo**. Moxfield starts at **$1/mo**. Both have years of brand equity and free tiers. |
| 7.7 | ROI-based subscriptions drive "near zero" churn | ⚠️ | Backwards. A subscription that makes a **measurable financial promise** is one the subscriber can **falsify monthly**. Performance-claim subscriptions churn *harder* than community or content subscriptions, not softer. |

## Scorecard

| Category | Count |
|---|---|
| ✅ Confirmed | 15 |
| 🟡 Partly true / overstated | 6 |
| ⚠️ Materially wrong | 10 |
| ❓ Unsubstantiated | 7 |

**Where the report is strongest:** the Altered TCG failure analysis, the identification of first-click/48-hour attribution as a binding constraint, and the Riftbound launch metrics — all accurate and genuinely useful.

**Where it is weakest:** every figure that supports a *strategic conclusion* rather than describing an event. The Pocket download count, the One Piece revenue figure, the Riftbound margin, the Asmodee attribution and the market sizing are each the load-bearing number in their section, and each is wrong, stale, or unsourced.
